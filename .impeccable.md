## Design Context

### Users
Event/festival attendees playing a collaborative storytelling RPG card game on their phones. Groups of 2–8 people at organized workshops. Context: busy event space, phones in hand (landscape mode strongly preferred), likely standing or sitting in a circle. Players may be nervous, excited, or new to the game. They need clear affordances and minimal friction.

### Brand Personality
**Handcrafted, Grounded, Hopeful.**

Solarpunk Futures is a physical card game digitised for events. The aesthetic references community zines, risograph printing, botanical illustration on recycled paper, and craft market letterpress. Think: the inside cover of a small-run political art book, not an app. Warm, cooperative, non-corporate.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Theme**: Dark ink welcome screen → warm paper tones throughout gameplay
- **Palette**: Earthy tones — ink (`#1a1008`), paper (`#f4ede0`), forest green, rust orange, gold, deep teal
- **Typography**: Space Grotesk (headings, uppercase bold), IBM Plex Serif (body), IBM Plex Mono (cards, labels)
- **Texture**: SVG grain filter overlay for risograph/zine feel
- **Cards**: 3D CSS flip animation, portrait format, type-coded by top accent band
- **Anti-references**: Nothing corporate, nothing SaaS, nothing with rounded-rectangle cards + generic drop shadows

### Design Principles
1. **Grounded, not slick** — rough edges, grain texture, and earthy colors over polish and gloss
2. **Accessible first** — minimum 44px touch targets, focus rings, reduced-motion support, Twemoji for Android
3. **No decoration for decoration's sake** — every visual element earns its place
4. **Dark screens for immersion** — welcome and card-draw screens use dark ink bg to create ritual atmosphere
5. **Content hierarchy via type weight/size, not color** — the card type stamp + top accent band communicates type; no left-stripe sidebars
